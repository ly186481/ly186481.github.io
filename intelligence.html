AI Analyst

A research interface where users ask business questions and receive analysis grounded in the platform's underlying data and models.

Example question

“What happens to construction demand if rates fall 50bp?”

The system retrieves the relevant historical relationships, runs a scenario, displays the estimated path and identifies the variables driving the change.

Output

Evidence → scenario → interpretation

Charts, assumptions, uncertainty ranges and a concise decision brief can be exported for strategy or management meetings.
